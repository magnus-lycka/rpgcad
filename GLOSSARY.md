# RPGCAD

Layouts for tabletop role-playing, primarily Traveller.

## Language

**Plan**:
The layout of a location, such as a building, starship, or city area, including all its levels.

**Level**:
A floor, deck, or other vertical layer of a plan.

**Campaign**:
A role-playing campaign with which plans can be associated.

**Model**:
The spatial layout being developed, with no fixed outer boundary. Paper size and screen size do not limit its extent.

**Grid square**:
A square representing 1.5 m × 1.5 m in the fictional space. At a print scale of 1:75, it measures 2 cm × 2 cm on paper.

**Print tile**:
A region of the model represented on one printed sheet. A model can span as many print tiles as needed.

**Wall**:
A barrier with a length and physical thickness, measured in metres. Its thickness extends equally to either side of its centre line.

**Door opening**:
A gap in a wall through which characters can pass.

**Observer**:
A character or other viewpoint with a position and sight range used to determine what can currently be seen.

**Point of view (POV)**:
The location from which an observer's visibility is determined.

**Visible area**:
The area currently unobstructed from an observer's position within their sight range.

**Explored area**:
The area already revealed during play, retained after it leaves current visibility.

**Remembered view**:
The last-seen appearance of an explored area that is no longer currently visible.

**Observation**:
What the party could see from a particular POV within its sight range, given the surroundings and door states at that time.

**Sight blocker**:
An object or boundary that obstructs line of sight. Being drawn on a plan does not by itself make an object a sight blocker.
