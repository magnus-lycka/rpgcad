Experiment: can rectangles in grid coordinates give us a useful, immediately editable Traveller plan? Edit the text to find out. This tests explicit geometry; it does not interpret natural-language requests yet.

Language reference
# Units: grid squares; decimals allowed.
sheet WIDTH HEIGHT
wall METRES
room X Y WIDTH HEIGHT "Label" [ANGLE]
door X Y WIDTH [ANGLE]
item X Y WIDTH HEIGHT "Label" [ANGLE]

X/Y are measured from the top left.
Angles are clockwise degrees, rotating
around the rectangle's centre.
Doors start at X/Y and rotate there.
wall 0.15 means a 15 cm wall.
Doors cut through walls and show a swing.
Items are furniture/equipment outlines.
Use ordinary double quotes for labels.
Rooms may share edges. Walls are centred
on their edges, consuming some interior.

Try: change the wall thickness, move a room by 0.25 squares, or rotate a console. Shared wall edges are merged visually; overlapping rooms are not automatically joined.

Default sheet: 280 × 200 mm at 1:75. Print at 100% / actual size, without headers or footers; check that one square measures 20 mm. Printable margins depend on your printer. SVG retains physical dimensions.
